1. Aesthetics: 1/6
Meets: Contrast. The dark text on the white background is easy to read.
Positive: I found the heading "Hi, I'm Rhys." clear because it's bigger and bolder than the text around it.
Suggestion: I found the page hard to look at because the images are huge and different sizes, and everything is squashed against the left edge. Use CSS to make the images the same size, and add padding and margin so there is space around the text.
2. Functionality: 2/5
Meets: Page load (the text and all four images appear) and Links (Projects, Skills and Contact show as links).
Positive: I liked that all the images loaded with no broken picture icons.
Suggestion: I found a sideways scrollbar at the bottom because the images are too wide for the page. Add max-width: 100% to your images so they fit the screen. I also couldn't tell if the navigation links go to the right sections, so test each one.
3. Usability: 1/4
Meets: Find Info. The menu is at the top, so it's easy to spot.
Positive: I found it easy to see who the page is about because "Hi, I'm Rhys." is at the top.
Suggestion: I found the text too small and squashed. Increase the font size to at least 16px and add space between the lines. Also make sure every image has alt text so people using screen readers know what each picture shows.
4. Bonus: 2/3
Meets: Engaging and Spelling.
Positive: I liked your bio because it sounds natural and tells me about your interests, like coding and pho.
Suggestion: I couldn't tell this is a CV or portfolio site for part-time work. Add a short line near the top saying you're looking for part-time work.
Total: 6/18
The content is good, but the layout needs CSS. Your later screenshots, with the dark nav bar, cream background, circle photo and project cards, are a big improvement and fix most of these problems.
